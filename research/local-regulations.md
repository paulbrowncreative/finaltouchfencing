# Local Fence Regulations: Source Notes

Pulled 2026-10-07 from official municipal documents. These are used for the service-area pages and the permit guide. Ordinances change, so the site tells readers to confirm with the city before building.

## St. Clair Shores (Macomb County)
Source: City of St. Clair Shores, Fence Permit Application (updated 2/23/2026), which includes Code Secs. 8-249 to 8-253 — https://www.scsmi.net/DocumentCenter/View/443/Fence-Permit-Application-PDF ; 311 FAQ — https://stclairshoresmi.qscend.com/311/knowledgebase/article/576
- Permit required. Community Development Dept, 27600 Jefferson Ave, 586-447-3340. Fee: residential $100, commercial $150, plan review $30 if applicable.
- A post-hole inspection and a final inspection are required.
- Posts set at least 42 in. below grade, embedded in concrete or by another accepted practice (Sec. 8-249).
- Privacy fences: max 6 ft 6 in; individual panels max 6 ft. The "bad side" faces the installer's property; max 3 in. gap at grade (Sec. 8-252(e)).
- Corner lots: max 6 ft 6 in along the side street to the front of the house, then 4 ft 6 in, sloping down to 2 ft 6 in at the front lot line. Material must not obscure vision (Sec. 8-252(b)).
- **Privacy fences are not permitted on any waterfront or canal lot** (Sec. 8-253(11)).
- No fence beyond the face of the house toward the front lot line without Board of Fence Arbitration consent (Sec. 8-253(10)).
- The application asks whether the property borders a lake, canal or river, and whether an existing fence is being removed. The city does not determine fence ownership.

## Harper Woods (Wayne County)
Source: City of Harper Woods Zoning Ordinance, Sec. 10-228 Fences and Walls — https://harperwoodscity.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/ZoningOrdi.pdf
- Front of the building: max 3 ft and ornamental in design. Rear and side yards: max 6 ft.
- Side-yard fences must sit behind the front foundation wall (whichever house's wall is farther back). Corner lots: max 6 ft on the street-side lot line, extending to the front building line.
- Corner clear-vision triangle: max 30 in. above curb level.
- Fences on the lot line require written consent of all adjacent owners (Sec. 10-228 C).
- The finished side faces out (D). Each fence must have its own posts, and fences can't share posts (F.3). No barbed wire or electric fences (G).

## Chesterfield Township (Macomb County)
Source: Chesterfield Twp Fence Application and Ordinance — https://www.chesterfieldtwp.org/DocumentCenter/View/2633/Fence-Application
- A zoning permit is required, fee $65. Electronic applications only since 1/1/2024. Needs a mortgage survey or plot plan, the size and material, the applicant's driver license, and the signed ordinance. Building Dept, 47275 Sugarbush, 586-949-0400.
- Side and rear yards: 3 ft to 6 ft above mean grade.
- Front yard: only decorative, non-obscuring split rail, 24 to 42 in.
- Waterside front yard on Anchor Bay or the Salt River (south of Callens Rd): only non-obscuring decorative aluminum or wrought iron, max 48 in. No walls, hedges, chain link or obscuring fences there.
- **No privacy fences outside the building envelope on any waterfront or canal lot.**
- Street-side yard fences are set back at least 5 ft from the right-of-way. 15 ft clear-vision corner triangle at driveways and roads.

## Macomb Township (Macomb County)
Source: Macomb Twp Fence Permit Requirements (rev. 10/12/2022) — https://www.macomb-mi.gov/DocumentCenter/View/7260/Fence-Requirements
- Permit fee $75. Two copies of the plot plan showing the fence location, lengths, type and height.
- The Water Dept does a grade check (about 2–3 days) for easements and grade changes. The permit then takes 7–10 working days. A final inspection is required.
- No double fencing (keep 3.5 ft from any other fence). No spiked or pointed tops. The finished side faces out.
- The township doesn't enforce subdivision restrictions, and some subdivisions don't allow fencing, so check the HOA.

## Clay Township (St. Clair County)
Sources: Clay Twp Building Dept — https://claytwpmi.gov/buildingdept ; Zoning Ordinance #126 (adopted Aug 20, 2007), Sec. 3.08 — https://cms2.revize.com/revize/claytownship/documents/docs/Clay_Township_Zoning_Ordinance_2007_july.pdf
- Fences need a Zoning Compliance Permit. 810-794-9320.
- Side and rear yards: max 6 ft. In front of the house or the required front yard: max 4 ft. No obscuring fence in the front yard. Decorative non-obscuring front-yard fencing up to 4 ft is allowed, but chain link doesn't count as decorative.
- At least one side must be "finished" and face adjacent properties.
- Materials: treated wood, plastic, aluminum, galvanized metal or similar. No chicken wire or snow fence as permanent fencing.
- This is the 2007 ordinance as posted. Amendments may apply, so the site tells readers to confirm with the township.

## Detroit (Wayne County)
Source: Detroit Historic District Commission, Fence and Hedge Guidelines — https://detroitmi.gov/government/commissions/historic-district-commission/historic-district-commission-general-scope-work-guidelines/hdc-site-improvements-fences-paving-and-landscaping
- Fence permits come from the Buildings, Safety Engineering & Environmental Department (BSEED). In local historic districts, applications are also reviewed by the Historic District Commission.
- The HDC guidelines don't allow stockade fencing. Other wood fencing, chain link, and aluminum that replicates wrought iron are allowed.

## MISS DIG 811 (statewide)
Source: https://www.missdig811.org/homeowners/faq.html
- Contacting MISS DIG 811 is required at least 3 business days before any digging, of any size or depth. It's free and open 24/7.
- If a contractor is doing the work, the contractor places the ticket.
- Digging must start within 14 days and finish within 21 days of the Work Legal Start Date.
- Hand-dig within 4 ft of the marks.
- Private lines (to a shed, sprinklers, invisible dog fences, a pool or a detached garage) are **not** marked by utilities.
