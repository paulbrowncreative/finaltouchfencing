# Final Touch Fencing: Voice and Style Guide

Apply this to every page, blog post, form message and listing description.

## Voice

**Plainspoken, local and confident.** We sound like the owner explaining the job on your driveway: specific, honest and no sales pitch.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Specific: "Posts set at least 42 inches deep" | Vague: "Top-quality craftsmanship" |
| Direct: "You need a permit in St. Clair Shores." | Hedging: "You may possibly want to consider…" |
| Helpful first, then we ask for the job | Pushy: "Act now!", "Don't miss out!" |
| Neighborly: "we" and "you" | Corporate: "The Company", "customers shall" |
| Honest about limits: "Confirm with the city" | Overpromising: "Guaranteed approval" |

- **Point of view:** "we" means Final Touch Fencing and "you" means the homeowner. Never refer to ourselves in the third person in body copy. The brand name is fine in headings, metadata and reviews.
- **Sentences:** keep them short. Lead with the answer, then the reason. One idea per paragraph.
- **Proof over adjectives:** cite the ordinance, the review or the photo. Never invent a statistic, award, year count or guarantee.
- **Banned phrases:** "Look no further," "Whether you're…," "Elevate your…," "We're passionate about…," "Top-notch," "Second to none," "Your one-stop shop."
- **Contractions** are fine (we're, you'll, don't).

## Mechanics

- **Headings (H1–H3):** sentence case, with no closing period. The one exception is the quote confirmation message.
- **Navigation, buttons and footer link lists:** Title Case ("Get a Free Quote", "Service Areas"). CSS uppercases buttons visually.
- **Lists in a sentence:** no serial comma (AP), unless leaving it out would make the list ambiguous.
- **"and" vs. "&":** write "and" in all on-page copy. "&" is allowed only in title tags, where space is tight.
- **Dashes:** em dash with a space on each side for breaks ( — ). En dash with no spaces for numeric ranges (24–42 inches, 2025–2026).
- **Numbers:** spell out one through nine and use figures for 10 and up. Always use figures for measurements, money, times, percentages and ages (6 feet, 3 inches, $65). The symbols ′ and ″ are allowed only in tables.
- **Compound measurements:** "a 6-foot fence" (hyphenated before a noun), "the fence is 6 feet tall."
- **Times:** 8 a.m.–5 p.m.; Monday–Friday.
- **Phone:** (810) 614-4181.
- **Quotes and apostrophes:** curly (" " ' '). Periods and commas go inside the closing quote mark.
- **Abbreviations:** "e.g." and "i.e." only in form hints. Use "vs." in titles.
